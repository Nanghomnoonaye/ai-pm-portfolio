        1. Yield Trend

        良率趨勢 · liánglǜ qūshì

        Why it matters

        During a ramp, management's #1 question is 'is yield climbing to target?' One day is noise — you need the trend.

        How (pandas)

        df = pd.read_csv("production_daily.csv", parse_dates=["date"])
df["yield"] = df.units_good / df.units_built      # a RATE, not a count
df["ma7"]   = df["yield"].rolling(7).mean()       # smooth daily noise

        Takeaway

        Yield rose 85.9% → 96.1% and crossed the 95% target mid-ramp. Healthy.

        2. First-Pass Yield by Station

        各站良率 · gè zhàn liánglǜ

        Why it matters

        The line has many stations. To lift overall yield you fix the WORST station first. FPY = % passing without rework.

        How (pandas)

        df = pd.read_csv("station_fpy.csv").sort_values("first_pass_yield")
worst = df.iloc[0]      # lowest FPY = your target station

        Takeaway

        Function_Test at 94.8% is weakest — a 1% gain there saves the most units.

        3

        3. Defect Pareto

        柏拉圖 · bólātú

        Why it matters

        The 80/20 rule: ~20% of causes make ~80% of defects. Fix those few and you win most of the improvement. THE classic factory chart.

        How (pandas)

        df = pd.read_csv("defects.csv").sort_values("count", ascending=False)
df["cum_pct"] = df["count"].cumsum() / df["count"].sum() * 100

        Takeaway

        6 of 10 causes make ~80% of defects — led by solder bridge, cold solder, GPU thermal.

        4

        4. Cycle-Time Bottleneck

        瓶頸分析 · píngjǐng fēnxī

        Why it matters

        A line runs only as fast as its slowest station. Speeding up any other station is wasted until you fix the bottleneck.

        How (pandas)

        df   = pd.read_csv("cycle_time.csv").sort_values("cycle_time_sec")
slow = df.iloc[-1]
line_rate = 3600 / slow.cycle_time_sec     # units/hr the line can do

        Takeaway

        Burn-in at 720s caps the line at ~5 units/hr — that's the real constraint.

        5. RMA by Cause

        退貨分析 · tuìhuò fēnxī

        Why it matters

        RMA = customer returns. These feed CAPA (corrective action) and show whether factory defects are escaping to customers.

        How (pandas)

        df = pd.read_csv("rma.csv").sort_values("returns", ascending=False)
# cross-check the top cause against the production defect Pareto

        Takeaway

        GPU thermal leads RMA (41), matching the production Pareto — a clear signal to focus on thermal/cooling.
